# NBA Rules — Plain-Language Summary

> Original summary written for this project. Paraphrased for demo/testing use, not
> an official document. Run `scripts/download_corpus.py` to index the real NBA
> rulebook and Collective Bargaining Agreement.

## Traveling

A player who receives the ball while standing still may pivot on one foot but may
not change that pivot foot. A player on the move may take two steps after picking
up the dribble before releasing the ball to pass or shoot. Lifting the pivot foot
and putting it back down before releasing the ball is a traveling violation, which
turns the ball over to the other team with no free throws.

## Three-second violation

An offensive player may not remain in the lane (the painted area between the
baseline and the free-throw line) for more than three consecutive seconds while
their team controls the ball in the frontcourt. The count resets when the player
clears the lane, when a shot is attempted, or when team control is lost. The
penalty is a turnover.

## Defensive three seconds

A defensive player may not stay in the lane for more than three seconds unless
they are actively guarding an opponent. The penalty is a technical foul: one free
throw for the offense and the offense retains possession.

## Shot clock

A team has 24 seconds to attempt a shot that hits the rim. After an offensive
rebound the shot clock resets to 14 seconds rather than 24. Failing to get a
shot to the rim in time is a shot-clock violation and a turnover.

## Personal fouls and foul-out

A player who commits six personal fouls in a game is disqualified and must leave
the game. Team fouls also matter: starting with a team's fifth foul in a quarter,
the opponent shoots two free throws on every subsequent non-shooting foul (the
"penalty" or "bonus").

## Flagrant fouls

A flagrant foul 1 is unnecessary contact. A flagrant foul 2 is unnecessary and
excessive contact and results in automatic ejection. Both types give the fouled
team two free throws and possession of the ball. Flagrant 2 fouls are reviewed
and can carry fines or suspensions.

## Technical fouls

A technical foul is called for unsportsmanlike conduct, delay of game, or certain
rule breaches that do not involve contact. It awards one free throw to the other
team. A player who receives two technical fouls in one game is ejected. Accumulating
16 technical fouls across a regular season triggers a one-game suspension, with
further suspensions for each two additional technicals.

## Clear-path foul

If a defender fouls an offensive player who has a clear path to the basket during
a transition, with no defender between the ball and the rim ahead of the play, the
offense gets two free throws and keeps possession.

## Goaltending and basket interference

Touching a shot on its downward flight, or after it touches the backboard on a
shot attempt, or while it is in the cylinder above the rim, is goaltending or
basket interference. On a defensive violation the basket counts. On an offensive
violation the basket is waved off and it is a turnover.

## Overtime

If the score is tied at the end of regulation, the teams play a five-minute
overtime period. Additional five-minute periods are played until one team leads
at the end of a period. Each team is granted two timeouts per overtime period.

## Timeouts

Each team gets seven timeouts for the full game, all the same length (75 seconds).
No more than four timeouts are allowed in the fourth quarter and no more than two
in the last three minutes of the fourth quarter.

## Coach's challenge

A head coach may call one timeout to trigger a challenge of a called foul, an
out-of-bounds ruling, a goaltending call, or a basket-interference call. If the
challenge succeeds the timeout is retained (since the 2023-24 rules a team that
wins its first challenge gets a second one).

## Play-In Tournament

After the regular season, the teams seeded 7 through 10 in each conference enter a
play-in tournament. The 7-seed hosts the 8-seed, with the winner taking the 7th
playoff spot. The 9-seed hosts the 10-seed; that loser is eliminated. The loser
of the 7-vs-8 game hosts the winner of the 9-vs-10 game for the 8th seed.
